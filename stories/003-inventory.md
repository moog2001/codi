# Inventory

*Contents of Storage Unit 114, Greenway Self-Storage, auctioned for nonpayment. Listed by the auction clerk, M. Ferrante, who was asked to be thorough.*

1. Cardboard box, labeled KITCHEN in blue marker. Contains: one kettle (dented), four mugs (one reading WORLD'S OKAYEST DAD), one cheese grater, a bag of rubber bands.

2. Cardboard box, labeled KITCHEN 2. Contains: a rolling pin, flour-crusted. A recipe card for "Nana's Lemon Bars" in old-fashioned handwriting, with a correction in newer handwriting: *less sugar. she always said less sugar and never did.*

3. Bicycle, child's, red. Training wheels removed; holes where they were.

4. Bicycle, adult's, blue. Front tire flat.

5. Bicycle, child's, larger, green. The red one's successor, presumably.

6. Twelve framed photographs, wrapped in newspaper. The newspaper is from March of the year the unit was rented. The photos have been turned face down. I did not turn them over. The instructions said to list contents, and I have listed them.

7. Box labeled DO NOT OPEN. I opened it. I am sorry. It contained a wedding dress, carefully folded, and a smaller box inside, which was empty, and which had been lined with velvet in the shape of a ring.

8. A desk lamp with no bulb.

9. A guitar, six strings, one broken. In the case: a set of new strings, still in the package, never put on.

10. Three garbage bags of clothing. Men's, mostly. One child's raincoat, yellow, with a duck on the hood.

11. A shoebox of letters, rubber-banded (see item 1, rubber bands). I did not read them. I would like this noted. I read the first line of the top one only, to determine whether they had value. It said: *I know you're not going to read this either.*

12. One snow globe, a city I didn't recognize, the snow all settled.

13. Taped to the inside of the unit door: a note, in the newer handwriting from item 2. It says: *Back for the rest in spring.*

The unit was rented eight years ago. Payments stopped after two.

Auction value, estimated: $140, mostly the bicycles.

I have been asked to recommend whether the contents should be sold as a lot or disposed of. I recommend the following: that the auction be postponed one more month. I recognize this is outside my authority. I would like the record to show that I shook the snow globe before I put it back, and that it snowed on the city, and that I watched it until it settled.
